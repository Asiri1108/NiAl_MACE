Step 8 - MACE versus Materials Project DFT Benchmark
============================================================================

1. Objective
------------
Quantify how the relaxed MACE-MP-0 Small formation energies and relaxed structures for the five selected Ni-Al phases differ from the Materials Project processed DFT-derived references.

2. Benchmark definition
-----------------------
The benchmark energy is the Materials Project processed formation_energy_per_atom from the summary endpoint. Raw MACE and VASP total energies were never compared.

9. Limitations
--------------
- Materials Project values are processed DFT-derived reference data under the Materials Project correction and mixing scheme, not experimental truth and not raw uncorrected DFT total energies.
- This five-phase benchmark is not a complete Ni-Al phase diagram.
- Materials Project energy above hull is computed against the full Materials Project Ni-Al entry set and is not directly comparable to the Step 7 selected-set MACE envelope.
- Raw MACE and VASP total energies are never compared.
- Sample size is five compounds; correlation metrics are exploratory and threshold counts are descriptive bins only.
- Ni is a magnetic element in DFT descriptions.
- The current structural MACE workflow does not provide an explicit user-controlled spin or magnetic-moment input.
- Step 7 therefore calculates the reference energy produced by the configured pretrained MACE model for the selected Ni crystal structure.
- This is a MACE-consistent reference, not a direct controlled magnetic DFT reference.
- Magnetic-state sensitivity must be considered during later DFT comparison.
