4. Formation-energy equation
----------------------------
formation_energy_eV_per_atom =
  (compound_total_energy_eV
   - N_Al * mu_Al_MACE_eV_per_atom
   - N_Ni * mu_Ni_MACE_eV_per_atom) / (N_Al + N_Ni)

8. Magnetic limitation for Ni
-----------------------------
- Ni is a magnetic element in DFT descriptions.
- The current structural MACE workflow does not provide an explicit user-controlled spin or magnetic-moment input.
- Step 7 therefore calculates the reference energy produced by the configured pretrained MACE model for the selected Ni crystal structure.
- This is a MACE-consistent reference, not a direct controlled magnetic DFT reference.
- Magnetic-state sensitivity must be considered during later DFT comparison.
